Jasmine —

Today you turn **eleven**.

Somewhere between Manchester and the sea, there is a reef that was waiting for someone kind enough to look after it. That someone is **you**.

You are not here to fight.  
You are not here to be the biggest or the loudest.  

You are here to **swim gentle**, notice the living things, and help the ocean breathe again.

When trash floats where it should not, you clean it.  
When a turtle pauses to watch you, that is trust — earned soft, never forced.  
When the reef feels a little brighter, that is because **you** were there.

This game is a birthday gift from Dad: a small ocean of care, wonder, and “you can do hard things kindly.”

**Happy Birthday, my Ocean Ranger.**

## Play right now

| | Link |
|--|------|
| **Main gift site** | **https://rowblocks-abyssal-quest.netlify.app** |
| Backup | https://cboon99.github.io/Rowblocks-Abyssal-Quest/ |

1. Open the link in **Safari** or **Chrome**  
2. Tap **Continue as Jasmine**  
3. Tap **Dive Home Reef**  
4. Swim · look · clean the trash · meet the turtle  
5. After enough cleans → **You did it!**

About **ten gentle minutes** to your first win.  
Your progress saves on **your** device — your own little reef journal.

> *Tip: landscape on a tablet feels nicest. If it ever feels slow → Settings → Medium quality.*

## How to move (when you’re ready)

| Desktop | Touch |
|---------|--------|
| **WASD** swim | Stick |
| Mouse look (click the sea) | Drag to look |
| Space / Shift — up & down | Up / Down |
| **E** observe · **F** clean | Observe · Clean buttons |

Take your time. There is no rush hour down here — only light, sand, and life.
